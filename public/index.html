Dummy tickets, answered

Dummy ticket for a visa — your questions

What is a dummy ticket for a visa?

A dummy ticket is a flight reservation you show as proof of onward travel for a visa application, without buying a full ticket. Horizon's version is a real reservation held in the airline's own system — verifiable, not a fake PDF.

Is a dummy ticket legal?

Yes. A genuine flight reservation used as proof of onward travel is completely legitimate. What isn't legitimate is a forged or edited ticket — which is exactly what Horizon avoids. Every reservation we issue is real and checkable.

Will an embassy or consulate accept a dummy ticket?

Most consulates and airlines accept a verifiable flight or hotel reservation as proof of onward travel, because they can confirm it in the airline's own system. No service can guarantee visa approval — that decision is always the consulate's.

How is Horizon different from a cheap dummy ticket PDF?

A cheap PDF only looks like a booking; enter its reference on the airline's site and nothing comes up. Horizon issues a genuine reservation with a booking reference that returns your name when checked — and a full refund if we can't issue on your route.

How much does a dummy ticket for a visa cost?

Flight reservations start at GH₵350 (about $29), hotel reservations at GH₵340, a flight-plus-hotel bundle at GH₵540, and a return flight at GH₵300 (about $25) — per traveller, with no route surcharges.

How fast is it, and how is it delivered?

Most reservations are ready within about two hours. You choose delivery by email, WhatsApp, or both, and you can track your order any time with your reference and email.
